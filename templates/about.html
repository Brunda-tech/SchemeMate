About SchemeMate

SchemeMate is a personalized government scheme discovery platform designed to help people find schemes and benefits that may be relevant to their situation.

Why SchemeMate?

Many government schemes are available for students, farmers, job seekers, entrepreneurs and other groups, but finding the right scheme can be difficult.

SchemeMate collects basic information about the user and uses eligibility rules to identify potentially relevant schemes.

How it works

Register → Select your roles → Provide your details → Discover relevant schemes.
